Tuition Khata ("the app") is a notebook for private tutors in Bangladesh. It
keeps your students, attendance and fee records. **Your records stay on your
phone.** We, the publisher ([PUBLISHER NAME]), do not receive them and cannot
see them.

## What the app stores, and where

On your phone only, in the app's private storage:

- Students: names, class, school, guardian name and phone numbers, address,
  notes, an optional photo.
- Batches and schedules, attendance, fees, payments and receipt numbers.
- Your own name, institution and phone number, if you enter them (they are
  printed on receipts you choose to share).
- Your settings, message templates, and, if you turn it on, a PIN protecting the
  app (kept as a salted, slow hash in the phone's secure storage, never as the
  PIN itself).

No other app can read this storage. Android's automatic cloud backup and
device-to-device transfer are switched off for the app, so these records are not
copied to Google or to a new phone by the system.

## What the app does not do

- It has **no internet permission**. It cannot send anything over the network.
- It has no account, login, advertising, analytics or crash-reporting
  libraries, and shares nothing with third parties.
- It does not read your contacts, location, messages, microphone or files.

## What leaves the phone, and only because you ask

- **Messages and receipts.** When you tap "remind guardian", "share receipt" or
  "share attendance", the app opens SMS, WhatsApp or the share sheet with the
  text or file ready. Nothing is sent until you press send in that app, and from
  then on that app's own policy applies.
- **Backups.** "Back up now" makes a file and opens the share sheet so you can
  keep it (Google Drive, WhatsApp, email, another phone). The file contains your
  records and photos. You can protect it with a password; without one, anyone who
  gets the file can read it. Keep backups somewhere private.

## Permissions the app asks for

| Permission | Why | When |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Class, fee and backup reminders | When you turn reminders on |
| Run at start-up | Restore reminders after the phone restarts | (automatic with reminders) |
| Biometrics | Fingerprint or face unlock for the optional app lock | Only if you turn it on |
| Vibrate | Notification vibration | With notifications |

## Your control

- Delete everything: Settings, Backup & restore, **Delete all data**. Uninstalling
  the app also removes it.
- Back up and restore your records at any time.
- Because we hold no copy of your records, we cannot recover them if you lose the
  phone and have no backup. Please back up regularly.

## Children's information

The app records school students' names and guardian contact details because the
tutor enters them. That information is the tutor's, stays on the tutor's phone,
and is not collected by us. Tutors should keep it as private as any class
register.
