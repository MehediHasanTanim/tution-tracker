## English

**Title:** Tuition Khata

**Short description (≤ 80):**
Students, attendance and fees for private tutors. Offline, in Bangla and English.

**Full description:**
A simple register for private tutors in Bangladesh. Put the paper notebook and the month-end sums behind you.

✔ Add a student in a minute: just a name and a fee
✔ Attendance in 20 seconds: everyone starts present, tap only the absentees
✔ Fees and dues at a glance, including part-payments and several months at once
✔ Receipts as a picture or PDF, ready for WhatsApp
✔ Remind guardians by SMS or WhatsApp with a ready-made Bangla message you can edit
✔ Monthly report: collected, outstanding, by batch
✔ Class and fee reminders, restored after a phone restart
✔ Back up and restore: everything comes back exactly on a new phone; add a password if you like
✔ Lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint

Privacy: your records stay on your phone. The app has no internet permission, no sign-up and no ads. If you never back up, a lost phone means lost records, so please back up regularly.

Works in Bangla or English, with Bangla or English digits, on small and older phones (Android 7+).

## Store settings

- Category: **Education** (alternative: Productivity).
- Tags: tuition, tutor, attendance, fee, register, Bangladesh.
- Contact email / WhatsApp: to be filled in by the publisher; also set at build time
  (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`, `SUPPORT_WHATSAPP`) so the app's Settings shows it.
- Privacy policy URL: the hosted copy of `privacy-policy.md` / `.bn.md`.
- Default language: Bangla (bn-BD); add English (en-US).
